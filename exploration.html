Explore Swimmers

Interactive exploration of Olympic swimming medalists (1900–2016)

How to Explore

Use the interactive plot below to examine 2,487 Olympic swimming medalists:

  • Hover over any point to inspect the athlete’s name, Olympic Games, event, medal, height, and weight.
  • Click or double-click stroke categories in the legend on the right to focus on a single discipline.
  • Click and drag to zoom into any cluster of interest, and double-click the plot area to reset the view.

Observations from the Data

  • Sprinters vs. Distance: Freestyle swimmers span the widest range, with sprint medalists clustering in higher height and weight ranges.
  • Stroke Differences: Breaststroke and Butterfly athletes exhibit dense clusters around 180–190 cm for men and 170–180 cm for women, reflecting the power demands of short-axis strokes.
